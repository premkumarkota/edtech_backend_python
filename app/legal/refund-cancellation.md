# Refund & Cancellation Policy

You can cancel your subscription at any time in the app. A first-time subscriber who isn't satisfied can get a full refund within 7 days.

## 2.1 How to cancel

- Go to Profile → My Subscription → Cancel Subscription and confirm. It takes no more than [2] taps, and you don't need to call or email anyone.
- Cancelling stops the next automatic renewal. You keep access until the end of the billing period you have already paid for.
- We confirm every cancellation by email or SMS within 24 hours.

## 2.2 7-day satisfaction refund (first subscription only)

If you are subscribing to [App Name] for the first time, you can ask for a full refund within 7 days of your first payment, as long as you have:

- watched no more than 25% of the recorded content in your plan, and
- attended no more than 2 live classes.

This applies once per learner. It does not apply to renewals.

## 2.3 Refunds we always give

Whatever the time or usage, you get a refund if:

| Situation | Refund |
|---|---|
| You were charged twice, or charged but the payment failed to activate your plan | Full refund of the extra or failed amount |
| You were charged a renewal after you cancelled | Full refund of that renewal |
| A booked live class didn't happen and wasn't rescheduled within 7 days | That class credited back, or a pro-rata refund |
| We stop a course or remove a major feature of your plan | Pro-rata refund for the unused period |
| The Platform is unavailable for more than 48 hours in a row because of a fault on our side | Pro-rata refund, or free extension, at your choice |

## 2.4 Multi-month and annual plans

If you cancel a quarterly, half-yearly or annual plan midway, we refund the unused full months, calculated at the plan's price per month. Any discount or free months received are adjusted. We don't charge any cancellation fee.

## 2.5 When refunds don't apply

- Monthly renewals after the billing period has started (you keep access until it ends).
- Accounts closed for breaking the Terms of Use or Community Guidelines.
- Change of mind after the 7-day window.

## 2.6 How to ask for a refund and how long it takes

- Request it in the app (Help → Payments → Request Refund) or email [Support Email] with your registered phone number and payment ID.
- We acknowledge your request within 48 hours and decide on it within 3 working days.
- Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 7 working days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
- We issue a GST credit note for every refund.

If you're not happy with the outcome, you can contact our Grievance Officer (Section 7).
