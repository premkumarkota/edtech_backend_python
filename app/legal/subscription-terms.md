# Subscription & Auto-Renewal Terms

Your plan renews automatically at the price shown at checkout until you cancel. We notify you at least 24 hours before every charge.

## 3.1 Prices and GST

- Plan prices are shown in Indian Rupees and include 18% GST, unless stated otherwise on the checkout screen. The checkout screen shows the full amount you will pay, with no fees added later.
- We send a GST invoice to your registered email after every payment. You can also download it from Profile → Payments.

## 3.2 Automatic renewal

- Monthly plans renew every month, and longer plans at the end of their term, on the same date you first subscribed.
- Auto-renewal runs only if you set up a payment mandate (UPI AutoPay, card or e-NACH) at checkout. You can turn it off at any time in Profile → My Subscription or in your UPI or bank app.
- As required by the Reserve Bank of India, we (or our payment partner) send you a pre-debit notification at least 24 hours before each renewal with the amount and date. Renewals above ₹15,000 need you to approve them with an OTP or PIN.

## 3.3 Free trials and offers

- If you start a free trial, we tell you the trial end date and the price that will be charged. We send a reminder at least 2 days before the trial ends.
- If you cancel before the trial ends, you will not be charged.
- A discounted price shows the regular price next to it. When the offer period ends, the plan renews at the regular price, which we show at checkout.

## 3.4 Price changes

We may change plan prices. For existing subscribers we give at least 30 days' notice in the app and by email. The new price applies from your first renewal after that notice. You may cancel before then without any charge.

## 3.5 Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, we retry for up to [3] days and notify you. If it still fails, your access pauses until you pay. We don't charge late fees.
