# Privacy Policy

We collect only the data we need to teach you, bill you and keep the Platform safe. We never sell your data, and we don't show targeted ads to students under 18. [Company Legal Name] is the Data Fiduciary for your personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

## 4.1 What we collect and why

| Data | Why we use it |
|---|---|
| Name, phone, email, class/grade, exam or subject interest | Create your account, match you to courses, contact you |
| Parent or guardian name, phone, email (for students under 18) | Get verifiable parental consent; send account and payment updates |
| Payment details: transaction ID, UPI ID, last 4 digits of card | Process payments, refunds and GST invoices. Full card details are handled only by our RBI-authorised payment partner |
| Learning activity: classes attended, videos watched, test scores, doubts asked | Track progress, show reports, apply the refund policy, improve teaching |
| Live class audio, video and chat | Deliver live classes; recordings for revision and safety reviews |
| Device and usage data: device model, app version, IP address, crash logs | Keep the app working and secure, prevent account sharing and fraud |

We don't collect Aadhaar numbers, contacts lists, or precise location.

## 4.2 Consent

- We ask for your consent when you sign up, in clear language, for each purpose above. Consent for marketing messages is separate and optional.
- You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawing consent for data we need to run your account means we can't continue the service. We will tell you that before you confirm.

## 4.3 Students under 18

- We don't allow anyone under 18 to use the Platform without verifiable consent from a parent or legal guardian, collected in the way the DPDP Rules, 2025 require (for example, an OTP to the parent's registered phone combined with an identity check).
- For students under 18, we don't track behaviour for advertising, we don't show targeted ads, and we don't process data in any way likely to harm the child's well-being.
- Parents can view their child's data, ask us to correct or delete it, and withdraw consent through the parent account or by writing to us.

## 4.4 Who we share data with

We share data only as needed, and under written contracts that require the same level of protection:

- Teachers: your name, class, progress and doubts, only for the classes you take with them. Teachers can't see your phone number, email or payment details.
- Service providers: payment gateway, cloud hosting, video-class provider, SMS and email providers, analytics and customer support tools.
- Authorities: when Indian law requires it, such as a court order or a lawful request from a government agency.

## 4.6 Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

- get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
- ask us to correct, complete or update it;
- ask us to erase it (except what the law needs us to keep);
- nominate a person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to;
- complain to our Grievance Officer, and if not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.

Make a request in Settings → Privacy or email [Privacy Email]. We respond within [30] days.

## 4.7 Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews. Only staff who need your data can see it. If a data breach affects you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Rules require.

## 4.8 Cookies and analytics

Our website uses essential cookies to keep you logged in, and analytics cookies only with your consent. You can change this in the cookie banner at any time.

We will notify you in the app before any material change to this policy. For privacy questions, contact our Grievance Officer (Section 7) or [Privacy Email].
