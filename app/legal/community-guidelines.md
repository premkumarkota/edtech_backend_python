# Community Guidelines & Live Class Conduct

Every class on [App Name] must be safe and respectful for students and teachers. Breaking these rules can lead to removal from a class, suspension, or account closure.

## 5.1 In live classes and chat

- Be respectful. No bullying, abuse, hate speech, sexual content, or comments about anyone's caste, religion, gender, body or background.
- Stay on topic. No spam, advertising or links to other apps or websites.
- Don't share personal contact details (phone, social media, address) in class chat or with teachers.
- Don't screen-record, photograph or share any class. Classes may be recorded by [App Name] for revision and safety; we tell you on screen when recording is on.
- Camera and microphone are optional for students unless the teacher asks for them for a specific activity.

## 5.2 Safety for students under 18

- Teachers and students talk only through the Platform's class, chat and doubt features. Private calls, video chats or messages outside the Platform are not allowed.
- One-to-one sessions with a student under 18 are recorded, and a parent may join or view them.
- If anyone makes you uncomfortable, use Report in the class or chat, or email [Safety Email]. We review every safety report within 24 hours and may suspend the account while we check.

## 5.3 Exams and tests

Don't share test questions or answers, or use someone else to take a test for you. Scores from tests where this happens may be cancelled.

## 5.4 Reporting and action

Anyone can report content or behaviour with Report or at [Support Email]. Depending on how serious it is, we may give a warning, remove someone from a class, suspend the account, close it, or report it to the police where the law requires.
